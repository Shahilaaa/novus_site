About NOVUS 3.0 Tech Fest

A celebration of talent, innovation and culture bringing together the brightest minds from across the nation.

Our purpose

NOVUS Fest 2026 is more than just a festival — it's a platform where innovation meets creativity, where technical prowess blends with cultural heritage, and where students from diverse backgrounds come together to celebrate their talents.

Our mission is to foster an environment of learning, competition and collaboration. Through a curated mix of technical challenges, cultural performances, workshops and sports competitions, participants get to showcase their abilities, learn new skills and create lasting memories.
